# Offer diagnostic

Diagnose the offer from evidence before recommending packaging or price.

Start with the market gate:

- the buyer is specific and reachable;
- the painful situation is concrete and current;
- purchasing power is supported rather than assumed;
- available signals do not show a shrinking or unsuitable market.

Then describe the draft offer in the customer's terms: desired outcome, why the buyer might believe it will work, expected time to value, required effort or sacrifice, and the smallest set of deliverables that addresses the painful problem. Treat price, conversion, willingness to pay, guarantees, scarcity, and urgency as assumptions until supported by relevant evidence.

Identify the binding weakness first: market, offer, reach, or economics. Do not compensate for a weak market or unsupported offer with more promotion. Record the exact evidence still needed, one next test, and what result would change the recommendation.

This framework supports diagnosis and drafting. It does not authorize outreach, spending, publishing, guarantees, or claims of customer demand.
