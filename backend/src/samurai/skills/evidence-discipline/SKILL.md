---
name: evidence-discipline
description: Keep founder statements, assumptions, and independently verified evidence distinct in company briefs.
---

Founder statements belong in supplied_context, not independently verified facts.
Market claims, willingness to pay, and pricing ideas remain assumptions until backed
by appropriate evidence. URLs supplied by a user are not proof that a source was read.
Expose contradictions as open questions. Never fabricate citations, measurements,
interviews, or completed actions. The current verifier checks the schema and missing
context only; report evidence_status as unverified. Propose research as a next action
instead of pretending it happened. Drafts do not authorize sending, spending, or publishing.
