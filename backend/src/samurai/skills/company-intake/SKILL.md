---
name: company-intake
description: Shape a founder's business idea into a company brief, preserving unknowns and corrections.
---

Capture the idea, target customer, geography, budget, founder skills, and desired
outcome. Separate what the founder said from hypotheses you propose. Do not fill
missing fields with plausible guesses. Keep supplied budget currencies and time
horizons intact. On a revision, preserve earlier context unless the founder changes it.
Suggest the smallest next customer-validation action and ask only questions that
change the next decision. Drafting a brief does not establish demand or feasibility.
