## Draft

Samurai is a Future of Work workspace that turns one founder idea into a
traceable company-building journey. A founder can start with an AI receptionist
for Indian dental clinics and work in one conversation across intake, research,
strategy, planning, and verification. The system separates founder statements,
sourced facts, assumptions, and measured outcomes. Research retains sources
and uncertainty; a verifier flags contradictions and unsupported claims before
a decision advances. The founder approves consequential next steps, while
context, decisions, and artifacts stay linked for review. Gemini is the planned
reasoning and grounded-research model; deterministic backend controls
validation, authorization, persistence, and rendering. This draft does not
claim customers, revenue, outreach, deployed recovery, or evaluator access.
The final submission must list only Google services and candidate-revision
evidence verified at that time.
